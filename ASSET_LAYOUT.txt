UniquePlayerRedirector does not ship Bethesda assets.
Place only the player-specific files you actually want to override.

Default mesh destination:

Data\Meshes\Actors\Character\PlayerCharacterAssets\
  FemaleBody.nif
  FemaleHands.nif
  1stPersonFemaleBody.nif
  1stPersonFemaleHands.nif
  MaleBody.nif
  MaleHands.nif
  1stPersonMaleBody.nif
  1stPersonMaleHands.nif
  ...any other skin-ARMA model whose upstream path is under CharacterAssets

Default female texture destination:

Data\Textures\Actors\Character\PlayerHumanFemale\
  FemaleBody_d.dds
  FemaleBody_n.dds
  FemaleBody_s.dds
  BaseFemaleHands_d.dds
  BaseFemaleHands_n.dds
  BaseFemaleHands_s.dds
  BaseFemaleHead_d.dds
  BaseFemaleHead_n.dds
  BaseFemaleHead_s.dds
  ...matching TXST files you want unique to the player

Default male texture destination:

Data\Textures\Actors\Character\PlayerHumanMale\
  BaseMaleBody_d.dds
  BaseMaleBody_n.dds
  BaseMaleBody_s.dds
  BaseMaleHands_d.dds
  BaseMaleHands_n.dds
  BaseMaleHands_s.dds
  BaseMaleHead_d.dds
  BaseMaleHead_n.dds
  BaseMaleHead_s.dds
  ...matching TXST files you want unique to the player

Notes:
- Mesh redirection preserves the upstream filename/subfolder after CharacterAssets.
- Normal skin texture redirection preserves the upstream filename/subfolder after
  BaseHumanFemale/BaseHumanMale.
- FaceBasenameFallback can also take a custom face TXST filename from another folder
  and look for that filename directly under PlayerHumanFemale/PlayerHumanMale.
- RequireLooseTarget=true means every missing destination automatically remains on its
  upstream path.
- Head meshes and FaceGen TRI/sculpt data are not redirected by this plugin.
